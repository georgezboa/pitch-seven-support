Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026. This policy describes Pitch Log version 1.0 for iPhone and iPad, provided by Zicheng Zhong. Contact: george.zboa@gmail.com.

Local storage

The app stores match videos, optional audio, team and player rosters, shirt numbers, match dates and venues, event tags, league results, imported videos, and custom event presets on your device. The core recording and review workflow uses local storage and direct device connections. There is no developer-operated account or cloud-storage service, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. We do not automatically receive your recordings or match data. Optional organizer-controlled league publishing is described below.

Camera, microphone and local network

Camera access records and previews matches. Optional microphone access adds pitchside sound; recording can work without audio. Local Network access discovers and connects your camera and control devices.

When you pair devices, the app sends live preview frames, recording commands, recording segment information, and match CSV/JSON between those devices over their local connection. App-generated device identifiers identify the camera and recording segments. Relative app timing enables preview latency measurement and event synchronization. These functions do not upload data to a developer-operated server. Use a local network you trust; pairing uses a six-digit code and the local transport does not add end-to-end encryption.

Optional league publishing

League publishing is off by default. An organizer may choose a league, supply their own HTTPS server address and publishing key, and explicitly enable it. The app then sends league and team names, fixtures, match dates and entered venues, scores, standings, event types and shirt numbers, and app-generated league/match/publisher identifiers to that chosen server. The publishing payload excludes original videos, audio, player names, private event notes, and complete rosters.

No publishing server is preconfigured or operated by the developer, and this feature is not needed to record, tag, or replay a match. The organizer controls the server and is responsible for its privacy, access and publication settings. The publishing key is stored in Apple’s Keychain on the device and authorizes requests only to the server the organizer specifies. Turning publishing off stops new uploads; copies already published remain on the chosen server and must be managed there.

Importing and exporting

The app reads CSV files and videos you choose in the system file picker. Exports go to destinations you select, which may include external storage or a cloud file provider. A selected provider handles those exported files under its own terms and privacy policy. The app does not automatically send exports to us.

Retention and deletion

Local league data is retained until you explicitly delete it or remove the app. Deleting a league or team keeps previously saved match snapshots and events. If you select “Delete originals after successful export”, original camera MOV files are deleted only after export and content verification succeed; match metadata is retained. Removing the app removes its local app data. Copies you export or send to other devices remain at those destinations, and should be managed there. Back up important recordings and records before removing the app.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate the issue, do not use them for advertising, and retain them only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the support address. Please avoid sending player details or video unless needed for your request.

Your responsibilities when recording

You control what you record and enter. Obtain appropriate permission from people whose images, voices, or details you record or share, including guardians where applicable. The app does not offer developer-hosted public profiles or social features. League data is published only after an organizer explicitly configures and enables optional publishing to their own server; the server may make that data publicly viewable.

Support website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its hosting provider may process normal web request information, such as your IP address, under its privacy policy. This website has no analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by Pitch Log.

Future changes

If future versions add accounts, servers, or data collection, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated to describe those changes. Additional permissions or choices will be presented where needed.
