Pitch Log Privacy Policy

Updated October 29, 2026.

For version 1.1 on iPhone and iPad, provided by Zicheng Zhong. This policy describes local functions and the integrated, optional online workflows. The pitchlog.cn website privacy policy also describes its account, league, analysis and storage practices.

Your choices and local use

You can create local matches, record video and optional audio, pair camera/control devices, tag events, replay linked local originals, and export your files without an online account. Videos, rosters, leagues, match events and presets are stored on your device. You choose imported files and export destinations. Selected file providers handle exports under their own policies.

Online league service and accounts

The app loads league information from https://pitchlog.cn by default and can use another valid HTTPS server you configure. The server receives network requests and may retain access/security records such as request time, IP address and requested paths. Existing website accounts provide access to content and actions according to their role. The app does not create an account. Passwords and verification codes are not saved in app files; a server-scoped session is stored in the system Keychain and cleared on explicit sign-out. Website account details, including email, display name, username, player identity and permissions, follow the website privacy policy.

Squads and match records

When an authorized user chooses to submit a team roster, the selected service receives the submitted player names, roster and match-kit numbers, starter/goalkeeper selections and related match identity. Imported website squads are saved to a local match after confirmation. Access to private pre-match squads follows the server permissions. Optional league publishing sends selected match/result information to the chosen server.

Optional recording uploads

Automatic recording uploads are controlled in Recording uploads and storage. With this option enabled and an authorized administrator account, completed originals, any recorded audio, and associated match metadata can be sent over Wi-Fi to the selected HTTPS server. Metadata includes stable match/player identities, event timing and an app-generated camera identifier. Transfers use resumable requests and integrity checks. Uploading does not automatically delete your local original. Upload authorization is stored in Keychain. Disabling uploads or explicitly signing out stops subsequent transfer work; copies already uploaded remain on the server.

Why information is used

Account and submitted data are used to authenticate users, enforce permissions, display and manage league/match information, associate events and recordings, provide replay/analysis and operate the service securely. We do not use app data for advertising, cross-app tracking, data-broker sales or third-party behavioral analytics. No advertising identifier is requested. No HealthKit or GPS location data is collected by the app.

Retention, access and deletion

Local files remain until you choose to delete them or remove the app. Explicit deletion of local video preserves match records and event timestamps, and does not delete server copies. Export cleanup occurs only after successful export and verification. Deleting the app removes its local data; exports and server copies must be managed separately. For pitchlog.cn, ordinary service access/security logs are configured to rotate and be retained for approximately 183 days. Uploaded recordings and associated records remain until an authorized operator removes them or handles an applicable deletion request. Consult the website privacy policy for account deletion, records and data-subject requests; the website provides account deletion under My account. A different server is managed by its own operator. Request deletion or correction of league/player information from that operator. Do not remove an app containing your only copy of a recording without a verified backup.

Recording people and young participants

Obtain appropriate permission before recording, uploading or sharing images, voices, names or match information, including guardian permission where required. Server account rules, age restrictions, access and moderation are described in that server's policy. Do not upload material you lack permission to share.

Camera, microphone and local network

Camera permission enables recording and preview. Microphone permission is optional. Local Network permission supports discovery, preview, recording control and match/event transfer between paired devices. App-generated identifiers distinguish cameras and recording segments. Pairing uses a six-digit code; the local device transport does not add end-to-end encryption. Use a trusted network and keep capture devices in the foreground.

Support and policy changes

If you contact support, we receive the information, email address and attachments you choose to provide and use them to respond and troubleshoot. Contact george.zboa@gmail.com for app support or a policy question. The support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may process ordinary website requests. The app privacy information and this policy must be updated when functionality or data practices change.
