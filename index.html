Pitch Log Support

Football recording, event-linked replay and native league information for iPhone and iPad.

Record and replay locally

  1. Keep both apps in the foreground on a trusted local network or the camera iPhone’s Personal Hotspot.
  2. On the camera iPhone, choose Camera and Start / resume camera. Allow Camera and Local Network; Microphone is optional. Note the six-digit pairing code.
  3. On the control device, create a local match, confirm each team’s roster, starters, goalkeeper and shirt/bib numbers, then connect to the camera.
  4. Start match and recording. Wait for REC and an advancing clock before tagging events. Each substitution asks for the incoming player’s bib or own shirt.
  5. Stop and save recording. Open Replay recording and events on the camera device and tap a linked event to play the local original. No CSV/video import is needed on that camera device.

Preview may use independently synchronized recording time when the control connection and timing remain valid. Events without reliable timing remain pending. Keep the camera app in the foreground and begin with 1080p. Locking or backgrounding the camera stops recording.

Online league data in version 1.1

Browse fixtures, results, standings, team/player information and available analysis. Online features use pitchlog.cn by default; detailed data depends on account permissions. Sign in with an existing website account to import permitted squads and confirmed starters. Local recording and replay work without an account.

Optional uploads and storage

An authorized administrator can enable resumable Wi-Fi uploads of completed recordings and match records to the selected HTTPS service. Uploading does not automatically delete the original. Export CSV/JSON and selected MOV originals; cancellation or failure preserves the originals. Keep a verified backup before deleting data. Local deletion does not remove server copies.

Language

My / 我的 → Settings / 设置 → Language / 语言 offers Simplified Chinese, English and System. New installations without a saved preference default to Simplified Chinese. Entered team/player names and saved match data retain their original text.
