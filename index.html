Pitch Log · iPhone & iPad

Your pitchside control room.

Record football matches on one device. Monitor the live preview and tag events on another. Replay event-linked original recordings on the camera phone.

Connect and record

  1. On the camera iPhone, open Camera mode and tap “Start / resume camera”. Allow Camera and Local Network access. Microphone access is optional.
  2. Connect the control device to the iPhone’s Personal Hotspot. Keep Wi-Fi enabled and both apps in the foreground.
  3. Create or open a match on the control device. Tap “Connect camera”, enter the camera’s six-digit code, and select it. If discovery fails, use the IPv4 address shown under “Local address”.
  4. Tap “Start match & record”. Recording starts remotely; the clock begins after the camera confirms a saved preview frame or synchronized recording time.

Tag and review

Select a shirt number, then a Goal, Shot, Foul, Card, or other event. Each tag saves match time and the position of the displayed recorded frame. If preview freezes while the control connection remains usable, tags use independently synchronized recording time; unavailable timing stays pending for manual association. Substitutions update the lineup. Use Review → Archive / switch match to reopen earlier matches.

Events sync back to the camera phone automatically. After stopping and saving the recording, tap “Replay recording and events” on that phone, then tap an event to jump and play the local original. No CSV or video import is needed on the recording phone.

To review footage on the control device, export original MOV segments from the camera device, import each matching file in Review, then tap an event to seek. Keep the original filenames and do not trim or re-encode the videos before importing.

Export and manage storage

Wait for “Match data saved on the camera device”, or use “Sync match data to camera”. In Camera mode, tap “Export videos + match data”. Select the videos you want and choose whether to keep originals or delete them after a verified export. Canceled or failed exports keep the originals. Exporting into another folder on the same device does not free device storage.

League data and language

Import a UTF-8 CSV with league,team,player_name,number headers to build a league database. Set each team’s players-on-pitch count separately. League records stay on the device until you delete them or remove the app. Existing matches keep their roster snapshots.

The app displays Chinese when your first preferred system language is Chinese, and English for other languages. Your own team names, player names, and notes remain unchanged.

Optional league publishing

Publishing is off by default and is not required for recording or review. An organizer can configure their own HTTPS server and publishing key, select a league, and enable automatic updates of scores, events and standings. Original recordings and player names are not uploaded by this feature. The organizer controls the server and any public spectator page; no server is preconfigured by Pitch Log. See the privacy policy for the information shared.

Before a match

Start with 1080p recording. Keep the camera powered, mounted horizontally, and the app visible. Locking the device or moving the app to the background stops recording. Preview uses a lower resolution than the local original recording. Bluetooth and a plain USB-C cable do not carry the preview; wired networking requires compatible Ethernet adapters.
